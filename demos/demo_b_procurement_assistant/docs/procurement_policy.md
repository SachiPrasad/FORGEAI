# Marine Procurement Policy

## Approval Thresholds
Purchase orders up to 5000 USD may be approved by the Chief Engineer. Orders between 5001 USD and 25000 USD require Fleet Superintendent sign-off. Orders above 25000 USD require Procurement Director approval and three competitive quotes.

## Emergency Vessel Spares
For critical vessel breakdowns, single-source procurement is permitted up to 15000 USD if lead time from primary suppliers exceeds 7 days, provided an incident report is attached.

## Warranty & Inspection
All critical main-engine and turbocharger spares must carry OEM or classification society certificates and include a minimum 12-month warranty from installation.
