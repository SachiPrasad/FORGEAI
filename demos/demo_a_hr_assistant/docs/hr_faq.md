# HR FAQ

## Annual Leave Requests
Submit paid time off requests in the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for trips longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 12 paid regional public holidays each calendar year.

## Benefits & Wellness
New hires must enroll in medical and dental benefits within 30 calendar days of start date. Full-time employees receive a 500 USD annual wellness stipend.
