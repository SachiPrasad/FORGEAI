Core Working Hours:
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Late Arrival & Remote Work:
Employees delayed by more than 30 minutes must notify their manager before 10:00 AM. Eligible staff may work remotely up to 2 days per week with 24 hours manager notice.
